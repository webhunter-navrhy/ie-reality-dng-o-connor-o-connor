About the property

Description property

3 Castlequarter, Clohamon Village, Bunclody, Co. Wexford

Superbly positioned on the Main Street of Clohamon Village, just a short 5-minute drive from Bunclody town centre, 1 Castlequarter offers a wonderful opportunity to acquire a modern and beautifully maintained home in a peaceful village setting.

Presented in immaculate, turn-key condition throughout, this end-of-terrace property features a bright and well-proportioned layout designed for comfortable family living.

Accommodation comprises of a welcoming entrance hallway, spacious living room, stylish kitchen and dining area, utility room, guest WC, three bedrooms, a modern ensuite, and a contemporary family bathroom.

The property benefits from on-street parking and rear access. The rear garden is fully concreted, offering a low-maintenance outdoor space ideal for everyday use.
